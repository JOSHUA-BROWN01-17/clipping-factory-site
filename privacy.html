Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Clipping Factory ("the app", "we") is a privately operated tool used by a single owner to upload approved short comedy clips to the owner's own YouTube channels, and to sort the owner's own business inbox (read-only). This policy explains what the app accesses, stores and shares.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube is a Google service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a channel through Google's official OAuth sign-in, the app asks for these permissions only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos the owner has approved to that channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's ID, handle and subscriber count (to confirm it is the intended channel), and the public view, like and comment counts of the owner's own posted clips.

The app does not read or use any other YouTube data: not your watch history, subscriptions, comments, other users' data, or analytics.

Gmail (read-only)

The owner can also connect the app's own business inbox, through Google's OAuth sign-in, with one permission only:

  • gmail.readonly: to read new emails in that one inbox, so the app can spot platform warnings (for example a copyright strike) and pause posting at once, and tell the owner about clip results, payouts and business enquiries.

The app cannot send, delete, label, archive or mark emails as read, and it only connects to the owner's own business address. It keeps only each email's sender, subject, Gmail's short preview snippet, the date and a one-line summary of what the email is about; it does not store the full email.

When simple rules can't tell what an email is about, its text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API only to sort it into a category and write that one-line summary. It is not used to train AI models, sold, or used for advertising.

Clipping Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app stores

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) for the connected channel, encrypted and stored only on the owner's own computer. Passwords are never asked for, seen or stored.
  • The connected channel ID and handle, and its subscriber count once a day.
  • For each of the owner's posted clips: its view, like and comment counts, saved once a day so the owner can see trends.
  • For each clip the app uploads: the resulting video ID, video URL and upload time, so the owner can keep track of their own posts.
  • For each email in the connected business inbox: sender, subject, date, Gmail's preview snippet and a one-line summary (never the full email).

This data is used only to operate the app for the owner. It is kept on the owner's computer and is not uploaded to any other server.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Video files and their titles/descriptions are sent only to YouTube, when the owner approves an upload. The only other service that receives any data is Anthropic's Claude API, for the email sorting described above.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not place cookies, tracking pixels or similar technologies on any device, and this website does not use cookies or analytics.

Retention and deletion

  • The connected channel's ID and handle are read again every time the channel is connected.
  • Video IDs and URLs of clips the app uploaded are kept so the owner can keep track of their own posts.
  • Email details (sender, subject, snippet, summary) are kept so the owner has a record of warnings, results and payouts; disconnecting the inbox stops all reading at once.
  • When the owner disconnects a channel or the inbox, its stored tokens are deleted immediately. If access is revoked in Google's settings, the stored tokens stop working at once.
  • To have any stored data deleted, email the contact address below.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
